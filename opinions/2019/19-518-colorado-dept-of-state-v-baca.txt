Per Curiam.
The judgment of the United States Court of Appeals for
the Tenth Circuit is reversed for the reasons stated in Chia-
falo v. Washington, 591 U. S. 578 (2020).
It is so ordered.
Justice Sotomayor took no part in the decision of this
case.
Justice Thomas concurs in the judgment for the reasons
stated in his separate opinion in Chiafalo v. Washington, 591
U. S. 578, 597–609 (2020).
