In 1991, Congress enacted a general restriction on robo-
calls to cell phones. In 2015, Congress carved out an ex-
ception that allowed robocalls made to collect government
debt. In doing so, Congress favored debt-collection speech
over plaintiffs' political speech. We hold that the 2015
government-debt exception added an unconstitutional ex-
ception to the law. We cure that constitutional violation
by invalidating the 2015 government-debt exception and
severing it from the remainder of the statute. The judg-
ment of the U. S. Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit
is affirmed.
It is so ordered.
