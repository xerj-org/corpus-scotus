 JUSTICE JACKSON, dissenting.
I would deny the stay. In my view, the applicants are not
likely to succeed on the merits of their argument that the
Fourth Circuit lacked statutory jurisdiction.  See Brown v.
FCC, ___ F. 4th ___, ___ (CA4 2026) (Wynn, J., concurring),
App. 55 (concluding there is statutory jurisdiction on con-
structive-denial grounds because “an agency may not re -
serve to itself the power to de feat judicial review through
delay or inaction”); see also Santos-Zacaria v. Garland,
598 U. S. 411, 417 (2023) (explaining that the Court “rou-
tinely” treats threshold requirements claimants must com-
plete before filing a lawsuit as  “nonjurisdictional” and has
“yet to hold that any statutory exhaustion requirement is
jurisdictional” (internal quotation marks omitted)).
